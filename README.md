A standalone ERP covering production, inventory, quality control, payments and AI-powered insights.

- **Mail OCR AI module:** extracts invoice data from vendor emails, performs three-way matching against purchase orders and goods receipts, and posts AP invoices to SAP Business One
- **Payments module:** NCHL integration, transaction validation, and reversal and refund handling
- **Production and quality control** modules for manufacturing workflows
- React interfaces and REST APIs backed by MongoDB

`React` `Node.js` `MongoDB` `SAP Business One` `NCHL` `OCR`
